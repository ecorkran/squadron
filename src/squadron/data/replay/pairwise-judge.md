The order of the drafts is arbitrary. Do not prefer a draft for being longer, for its formatting, or for coming first.

Write a short rationale of a few sentences that names the specific differences that decided it. Then end your reply with one final line of the form `WINNER: X`, where X is `A` if Draft A is better, `B` if Draft B is better, or `TIE` if neither is meaningfully better.
